### q09. How much did revenue change each year compared with the year before, as a percentage to one decimal place?

```sql
WITH y AS (
    SELECT strftime('%Y', InvoiceDate) AS year, SUM(Total) AS revenue
    FROM Invoice GROUP BY year
), change AS (  -- filter after LAG, or 2010 has no previous year
    SELECT year, ROUND(100.0 * (revenue - LAG(revenue) OVER (ORDER BY year))
                       / LAG(revenue) OVER (ORDER BY year), 1) AS yoy_pct
    FROM y
)
SELECT year, yoy_pct FROM change WHERE yoy_pct IS NOT NULL ORDER BY year
```

| year   | yoy_pct   |
|--------|-----------|
| 2010   | 7.1       |
| 2011   | -2.5      |
| 2012   | 1.7       |
| 2013   | -5.6      |
